1️⃣  WHAT IS THE MEETING SCHEDULER BOT?

       ▪ Maintains audit trail for compliance

2️⃣  HOW AI AGENTS WORK IN THIS PROJECT

SECTION A: WHAT IS AN AI AGENT?

An AI agent is a software program that:

    1. Perceives its environment (emails, meetings, user responses)
    2. Makes intelligent decisions based on data
    3. Takes actions automatically (sends emails, schedules meetings)
    4. Learns from interactions to improve over time

The KFSHRC Meeting Bot is an AI Agent because it:

    1. PERCEIVES → Reads incoming meeting requests and email responses
    2. THINKS → Analyzes all proposed times and counts votes
    3. DECIDES → Selects the best meeting time based on consensus
    4. ACTS → Sends confirmations, creates Outlook meetings, generates links


SECTION B: WHAT MODEL DID WE CHOOSE AND WHY?

We used Gemini AI (by Google - AI Foundation Model) for this project because:

    1. Fast and Efficient Processing
       ▪ Ideal for real-time email handling
       ▪ Responses within seconds
       ▪ Scalable for organization-wide use

    2. Strong Language Understanding
       ▪ Interprets emails naturally
       ▪ Understands context and intent
       ▪ Handles multiple languages/formats

    3. Reliable Decision-Making
       ▪ Selects best meeting times accurately
       ▪ Counts votes correctly
       ▪ Finds consensus fairly

    4. Cost-Effective
       ▪ Processes many requests without high overhead
       ▪ Efficient resource usage
       ▪ Economical for organizations


SECTION C: WHAT IS A FOUNDATION AI MODEL?

A Foundation Model is a large language model trained on massive amounts of text data.

Capabilities:
    1. Understand human language (written requests, responses)
    2. Generate human-like responses (write professional emails)
    3. Reason logically (analyze data, make decisions)
    4. Adapt to different tasks (scheduling, responding, formatting)

Gemini AI is a Foundation Model because:
    1. Trained on billions of words
    2. Can understand context and nuance
    3. Can perform many different tasks
    4. Can be guided by instructions (prompts)

Why It Matters:
Foundation models are the BRAIN of AI agents.
    ▪ The agent perceives emails
    ▪ The foundation model understands their meaning
    ▪ The agent decides what to do
    ▪ The foundation model generates the response

4️⃣  HOW TO USE (STEP-BY-STEP)

STEP 1️⃣: SEND MEETING REQUEST

Send an email to your Outlook inbox with:

    From:     Your email
    To:       Your email (or send to yourself)
    Subject:  meeting bot - [Meeting Title]

    Body:
    Attendees: email1@kfshrc.edu.sa, email2@gmail.com, email3@example.com
    Topic: Project Review Meeting
    Duration: 60
    Deadline: 10-08-2026 17:00 (optional)

Example Email:

    Subject: meeting bot - Q3 Planning Session

    Attendees: haninksa15@gmail.com, consultant@company.com, manager@kfshrc.edu.sa
    Topic: Quarterly Planning Session
    Duration: 90
    Deadline: 12-08-2026 18:00

✓ Bot will automatically:
    1. Create a meeting ID
    2. Send surveys to all attendees
    3. Start listening for responses


STEP 2️⃣: ATTENDEES RECEIVE SURVEY

Attendees will receive an email:

    Subject: Bot Test Meeting
    From:    Meeting Scheduler Bot

    Body:
    You have been invited to the following meeting:

    Topic: Quarterly Planning Session
    Organizer: haninksa15@gmail.com
    Meeting ID: a1b2c3d4

    Please reply to this email with the times you are available:
    Format: DD-MM-YYYY HH:MM

    Example reply:
    10-08-2026 14:00
    11-08-2026 10:00
    12-08-2026 15:30


STEP 3️⃣: ATTENDEES REPLY WITH AVAILABILITY

Each attendee clicks REPLY and enters times:

    10-08-2026 14:00
    11-08-2026 10:00
    12-08-2026 15:30

⏱️ They must reply within 12 minutes (or by deadline)


STEP 4️⃣: BOT ANALYZES & SCHEDULES

Bot automatically:

    1. Collects all responses
    2. Counts votes for each time
    3. Selects time with most agreement
    4. Generates Teams meeting link
    5. Generates Calendar links
    6. Sends confirmation to EVERYONE


STEP 5️⃣: EVERYONE RECEIVES CONFIRMATION

Confirmation email includes:

    Subject: Meeting Confirmed - 10-08-2026 at 14:00

    MEETING CONFIRMED

    Your meeting has been scheduled based on consensus availability.

    MEETING DETAILS:
    1. Topic: Quarterly Planning Session
    2. Date: Wednesday, August 10, 2026
    3. Time: 14:00 (90 minutes)
    4. Organizer: haninksa15@gmail.com
    5. Meeting ID: a1b2c3d4

    ATTENDEES (3):
    1. user@gmail.com
    2. consultant@company.com
    3. manager@kfshrc.edu.sa

    QUICK LINKS:

    1. JOIN TEAMS MEETING:
       https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meetup-join/a1b2c3d4

    2. ADD TO GOOGLE CALENDAR:
       https://calendar.google.com/calendar/render?action=TEMPLATE&...

    3. ADD TO OUTLOOK CALENDAR:
       https://outlook.live.com/calendar/0/deeplink/compose?...


STEP 6️⃣: ATTENDEES JOIN THE MEETING

Everyone can:

    1. Click Teams link to join instantly (no password!)
    2. Click Calendar link to add to their calendar
    3. Meeting is automatically created in Outlook

7️⃣  TROUBLESHOOTING

🔴 PROBLEM 1️⃣: Bot doesn't recognize my meeting request

    ✅ SOLUTION:
       1. Check subject line contains "meeting bot"
       2. Make sure format is exactly:
          - Attendees: email1@domain, email2@domain
          - Topic: Meeting Title
          - Duration: number of minutes
       3. Subject example: "meeting bot - My Meeting Title"


🔴 PROBLEM 2️⃣: I didn't receive a survey email

    ✅ SOLUTION:
       1. Check spam/junk folder
       2. Check if you're blacklisted (ask administrator)
       3. Make sure bot is running
       4. Wait up to 1 minute for email to arrive


🔴 PROBLEM 3️⃣: My reply wasn't detected

    ✅ SOLUTION:
       1. Reply to bot's email (not forward)
       2. Keep subject line with (ID: ...)
       3. Format times exactly: DD-MM-YYYY HH:MM
       4. One time per line
       5. Don't include other text with times


🔴 PROBLEM 4️⃣: Classification popup appears when sending

    ✅ SOLUTION:
       1. Click OK to dismiss (auto-click should handle)
       2. Email will go through after popup
       3. If repeated, upgrade pywinauto:
          python -m pip install pywinauto --upgrade


🔴 PROBLEM 5️⃣: Time zone issues

    ✅ SOLUTION:
       1. Always use local Saudi Arabia time (Arabia Standard Time)
       2. Example: 10-08-2026 14:00 (2:00 PM Saudi time)
       3. Bot uses your system time zone

8️⃣  BEST PRACTICES

✅ DO:

    1️⃣  Be clear in meeting title
        Good: "meeting bot - Q3 Budget Review"
        Bad:  "meeting bot - stuff"

    2️⃣  Give reasonable deadline
        Good: "Deadline: 10-08-2026 17:00"
        Bad:  5 minutes is too short

    3️⃣  Invite right people
        Good: Only people who actually need to attend
        Bad:  Each extra person slows consensus

    4️⃣  Propose realistic times
        Good: Within work hours (9 AM - 5 PM)
        Bad:  Past dates or unrealistic times

    5️⃣  Reply promptly
        Good: Respond quickly
        Bad:  Delays slow down scheduling


❌ DON'T:

    1️⃣  Forget "meeting bot" in subject
        ✗ Bot won't recognize it!

    2️⃣  Use weird time formats
        ✗ WRONG: 10/08/26 2:30 PM
        ✓ RIGHT: 10-08-2026 14:30

    3️⃣  Reply to old emails
        ✗ Always reply to latest survey

    4️⃣  Propose past dates
        ✗ Use future dates only

    5️⃣  Invite too many people
        ✗ More people = harder consensus

    6️⃣  Use invalid email formats
        ✗ Check for typos!
